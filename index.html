From plant breeding to genome-scale questions.

My academic background began in plant breeding and expanded into genetic engineering, bioinformatics, and genomics. I am interested in connecting genetic variation with measurable crop traits through both computational and laboratory approaches.

RESEARCH

Questions I am working on.

My graduate research integrates gene curation, comparative genomics, variation analysis, and starch phenotyping in cassava.

MSc RESEARCH · 2026—PRESENT

Pangenomic Insights into Starch Biosynthesis and Phosphorylation Genes in Cassava

Investigating sequence and structural variation in cassava starch-related genes and connecting genomic diversity with starch quality and phosphorylation phenotypes.

Computational

Target-gene curation, whole-genome analysis, variant calling, haplotypes, structural variants, and pangenomic comparison.

Phenotyping

FT-NIR, starch isolation, total phosphate analysis, G3P/G6P quantification, enzymatic assays, and microscopy.

Goal

Prioritize biologically meaningful genetic variation associated with cassava starch properties for further validation.
